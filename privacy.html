Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 7, 2024

AutoHub Kenya is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya) and guidelines from the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoHub Kenya ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects your personal data when you use our online marketplace platform. We are registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) and comply with all applicable data protection laws in Kenya.

3. What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

3.1 Information You Provide:

  • Registration Data: Full name, email address, phone number, National ID/Passport number, county of residence
  • Account Data: Username, password (encrypted), account preferences
  • Communication Data: Messages, inquiries, feedback sent through our platform
  • Transaction Data: Vehicle inquiries, saved searches, wishlist items

3.2 Information Automatically Collected:

  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on site, click patterns, search queries
  • Cookie Data: Session identifiers, user preferences, viewed vehicles (see Cookie Policy below)

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, we process your personal data based on:

  • Consent: You have given explicit consent for specific processing purposes
  • Contract: Processing is necessary to fulfill our services to you
  • Legal Obligation: We must comply with Kenyan laws (e.g., identity verification)
  • Legitimate Interests: For fraud prevention, security, and service improvement

5. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Providing and managing our car marketplace services
  • Processing your registration and maintaining your account
  • Verifying your identity in compliance with Kenyan regulations
  • Connecting you with vehicle sellers and dealers
  • Sending service updates and notifications about your account
  • Personalizing your experience and showing relevant vehicles
  • Analyzing usage patterns to improve our services
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring platform security
  • Complying with legal obligations and law enforcement requests
  • Sending marketing communications (only with your consent)

6. Data Protection & Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive information (passwords, ID numbers)
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access controls limiting who can view your data
  • Staff training on data protection obligations
  • Incident response procedures for data breaches

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the ODPC within 72 hours as required by law.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your personal data with:

  • Vehicle Dealers & Sellers: When you inquire about a specific vehicle
  • Service Providers: Hosting, analytics, payment processing (under strict data processing agreements)
  • Legal Authorities: When required by Kenyan law or court order
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

We do not sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary:

  • Active Accounts: Duration of account + 6 months after account closure
  • Transaction Records: 7 years (as required by Kenyan tax laws)
  • Marketing Consent: Until you withdraw consent
  • Legal Requirements: As mandated by Kenyan law

After retention periods expire, we securely delete or anonymize your data.

9. Your Rights Under Kenya's Data Protection Act

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with the ODPC

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@autohub.co.ke

10. Cookie Policy

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience:

Types of Cookies We Use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for site functionality (session management, security)
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and choices
  • Analytics Cookies: Understand how you use our site to improve services
  • Marketing Cookies: Track viewed vehicles for personalized recommendations (requires consent)

Cookie Information Stored:

  • Session ID for authentication
  • User preferences (language, display settings)
  • Recently viewed vehicles
  • Wishlist and comparison data
  • Cookie consent choices

Managing Cookies:

You can manage cookie preferences through:

  • Our cookie consent banner (shown on first visit)
  • Your browser settings (most browsers allow blocking cookies)
  • Account settings (for registered users)

Note: Disabling essential cookies may limit site functionality.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data is primarily stored and processed in Kenya. If we transfer data internationally, we ensure:

  • Adequate safeguards are in place (Standard Contractual Clauses)
  • Compliance with ODPC guidelines on cross-border transfers
  • Recipient countries have adequate data protection laws

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18 years. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we discover such collection, we will promptly delete the information.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of material changes via email or prominent site notice. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance.
